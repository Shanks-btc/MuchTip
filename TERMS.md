## How tips work

- A tip is held by the TipEscrow smart contract, not by us. We never hold your DOGE or your keys.
- A tip is locked to the X account behind the handle at the moment you send it. Check the account shown before you
  confirm.
- The person tipped claims by signing in with X. Claiming costs a network fee, paid from the receiving wallet.
- If a tip is not claimed within the refund window shown in the app, the sender can take it back.
- Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. Network fees are paid to the network and are not refundable.

## Claim signatures

To claim, our server confirms which X account you signed in with and signs a one-time permission that the contract
checks. On this testnet deployment that signing key is a test key operated by the MuchTip maintainer.
