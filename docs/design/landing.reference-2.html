Why this exists

Dogecoin was born for tipping.The tip bot just held the coins

In 2014 a Reddit bot made DOGE the internet's favourite way to say thanks: tens of millions of coins across hundreds of thousands of tips. But one company held everyone's balances, and when it went broke, users' coins went with it. MuchTip keeps the fun and removes the custodian.

What went wrong

Now · MuchTip
Tips held by open code
Sources: Benzinga · CoinDesk · Gizmodo

Why MuchTip can't
lose your DOGE

Custody

No company holds your coins

Tips sit in an open smart contract on DogeOS. There's no wallet for anyone to drain and no balance for anyone to spend.

Refunds

Unclaimed? It comes back

If the @handle never claims, you can take your tip back after the refund window. Nothing gets stuck forever.

Identity

Only the real @handle can claim

Tips are tied to the account's permanent X ID, not its name, so a renamed or recycled handle can't grab old tips.
