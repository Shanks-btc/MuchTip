MUCHTIP · DESIGN SPEC v3 · FOR IMPLEMENTATION

MuchTip premium redesign — every screen and state in one file

Approved mockups. Treat layout, copy, spacing, colours, type and states as the spec. Values such as balances, X IDs, tx hashes and counts are illustrative — the app must read them from wagmi, the TipEscrow contract, Supabase (resolve, sign-claim) and feed(). Never ship demo values as live data.

Colour

  • Gold #F2B824 — hero, final CTA, primary action
  • Ink #0E0F14 — text, borders on key surfaces, dark sections
  • Paper #FAFAF7 — page · White #FFFFFF — cards
  • Hairline #E7E5DE / #E0DED6 — dividers, card borders
  • Gold-ink #8A6400 — eyebrows & links on light
  • Success #16A34A / #0F6A31 on #E7F6EC
  • Error #B42318 on #FDECEA · Warning #8A3B0A on #FFF7ED

Type

  • Inter 700 headlines, tracking −0.035 to −0.045em
  • Inter 400–600 UI and body (15–19px)
  • JetBrains Mono for amounts, hashes, addresses, eyebrows (11–14px, 0.06em)
  • Numbered eyebrows: “01 — HOW THE MONEY MOVES”

Components

  • Buttons: 40 (nav) / 48–54px (primary), radius 10–12px, not pills
  • Inputs: 48–50px, 1px ink border, gold focus ring 3px
  • Cards: radius 14–16px, 1px border, hero card has ink border + deep shadow
  • Badges: 24–26px, radius 6–7px, dot + label
  • Max width 1280px, 32px gutter (16px mobile)

Motion

  • Fast and functional only: spinners, skeleton shimmer, 300ms fade-in on resolve, live-dot pulse, dashed flow line
  • Hover: 1px lift on buttons, row tint on tables, border darken on cards
  • All motion off under prefers-reduced-motion

01 — HOW THE MONEY MOVES

Every tip takes one of two paths. Both end with the DOGE in a real person's wallet.

Three contract calls cover the whole lifecycle: tip, claim and refund.

Your wallet

STEP 2 · TIPESCROW
Held by the contract

refund in 6:02

Nobody can move it except the verified recipient, or you after the refund window.

03 — WHY TRUSTLESS

Dogecoin was built for tipping. The old tip bot held everyone's coins.

In 2014 a Reddit bot made DOGE the internet's way to say thanks. One company held every balance, and when it ran out of money, users lost their coins. MuchTip keeps the tipping and removes the company in the middle.

2014 · TIP BOT
Balances in a company database
NOW · MUCHTIP
Balances in an open contract

Sources: Benzinga · CoinDesk · Gizmodo

  • 01
    Non-custodial escrow

    Tips sit in TipEscrow, not in a company wallet. There is no balance for anyone to spend.

    Contract ↗
  • 02
    Unclaimed tips come back

    After the refund window, the tipper can call refund and the DOGE returns to their wallet.

    How ↓
  • 03
    Tied to a permanent X ID

    Tips are keyed to the account’s numeric X ID, so a renamed or recycled handle can’t claim old tips.

    FAQ ↓
  • 04
    Every move is on-chain

    Tips, claims and refunds are contract events anyone can look up on the DogeOS explorer.

    Activity ↑
  • 05
    Open, tested contract

    The source is public and covered by 54 tests, including fuzzing and invariant checks.

    GitHub ↗
